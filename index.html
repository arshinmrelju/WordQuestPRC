How to Play Word Quest

Find all hidden words & beat the clock to score top rank

01

Scan the Grid

Locate the hidden English vocabulary words scattered across the letter matrix — every level adds more words and a bigger grid, but the 5-minute timer never shrinks!

02

Drag to Select

Click or touch and drag across letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to form words.

Rules & Regulations

Please read and accept all terms to participate

01

Game Format

This is not a level-based winning game. The winner is determined based on the total score earned during gameplay.

02

Speed = Score

Speed matters. The faster a player completes the game, the higher their potential score.

03

Winner Selection

The winner will be selected based on the highest score on the official leaderboard. The player with the highest final score will be declared the winner.

04

Game Timing

The game will be available from 10:00 AM to 3:30 PM. Additional time will be provided only in the event of a tie to determine the winner.

05

Final Decision

The organizers' decision regarding scoring, timing, leaderboard, and the final winner will be considered final and binding.
